Colophon

Schemata Musicae is Liber Secundus of De Harmonia Restituta, and carries the schematics, circuits, figures and tables belonging to that treatise. Thirteen plates in all: two of the machine entire, one living, nine of the canon, and one worked example.

The living plates compute apparent geocentric ecliptic longitudes for the seven classical bodies in the reader's own browser, and detect aspects at the orb tolerance the engine uses. Positions are reckoned to within a few arc‑minutes, which is well inside the eight‑degree gate; the machine itself reads de440s.bsp through Skyfield and is exact. The twelve waveforms of Plate VI are summed from the same amplitude series the engine hands to Buffer.sine1, and the sounds these plates make are a faithful transcription of the SynthDefs of Plates IX and X into the browser's own audio graph.

Set in the Fell types, cut for the University of Oxford in the 1670s and given to it by Bishop John Fell — the nearest living type to the presses that printed Fludd at Oppenheim — with Spectral for the reading text and IBM Plex Mono for the machine's own voice. The colours throughout are not chosen; they are the engine's, lifted from PLANET_COLORS, ASPECT_COLORS and MODE_COLORS so that a reader who has watched the stream will recognise every line.

Dedicated, as the first book is dedicated, to Robert Fludd of Bearsted.
